# M4 — Full Edit Creation, Storyboard, Versions, Photos in Edits

## Goal

Produce a credible automatic travel edit with real user control over the result.

## Scope

The screens in this milestone are listed with "M4" in the `docs/ui/README.md` screen index: S13 full, S14 full, S15, S16, S17 findings, S18, S19 and S24.

- **Full wizard** per `PRODUCT.md §4`: content weights, editing rules, pace, transitions (cuts, dissolves, dips), audio options, and a basic music bed (user track, fades, static level, simple ducking under detected speech).
- **Trip context in editing:** beats from itinerary days, must-include and avoid, and titles only from context. Minimal title cards (opening, day cards, closing) in one modern style.
- **Storyboard:** beat cards with reorder, lock and regenerate per beat.
- **Solver:**
  - lock constraints
  - photo `still` events with pan/zoom motion
  - blurred-fill for orientation mismatch
  - photo share preference
  - infeasibility reporting (for example, "locked shots exceed target by 22 s")
- **Cut refiner v2:** J-cuts and L-cuts for dialogue (audio lead/lag up to 1 s) and speech-aware in/out.
- **Deterministic critic** with findings in the UI. The **AI critic (optional)** takes the EDL plus summaries and returns structured suggestions. It does not analyze the rendered video in v1.
- **Findings UI:** Apply all, apply individually, Ignore. Each applied finding creates a child version.
- **Drafts and versions:** draft op log with undo/redo, immutable versions, version compare (event list diff and side-by-side preview), and revert.
- **Regeneration granularity** per `PRODUCT.md §8`.
- **Non-native aspect ratios:** center crop, or subject crop from vision observations with smoothing. This is not full smart reframing.
- **Final render profiles:** H.264 and HEVC via hardware encoders, 1080p/4K, and a ProRes 422 master where the encoder is available. Exports include EDL JSON, SRT captions from the transcript, and a selection/rejection report.

## Out of scope

- Beat-synced music (M6).
- Timeline editor with trim handles (M8).
- OTIO/FCPXML (M9).
- Raw 360 reframing.

## Acceptance tests

1. The v3 benchmark request (a 5-minute cinematic film, mostly chronological, wildlife, people and reactions, drone establishing shots, no shaky or repetitive shots, natural audio, user music track) on a real 2–4 hour mixed-camera trip produces an edit with every zero-target metric at 0 and a human rubric average of 3.5 or higher.
2. After a user locks 3 shots and regenerates the beat, the locked shots stay unchanged at the same relative positions in the new version.
3. "Make it 90 seconds" creates a new version within tolerance, re-using analysis. No vision calls are made; this is verified.
4. A photo-heavy request (photo share "many") mixes stills and video with no orientation artifacts.
5. Reverting to v1 after 5 versions yields a render identical to the original v1 render (chunk cache hash match).
6. All M4 screens match `docs/ui/reference/` in both themes and have a Storybook story for every listed state.
7. Help me label (S24) creates only `person_link` and context records. A test asserts that no face or biometric data is stored.

## Gate

**G6.** This is the final gate for v1. The owner watches the benchmark edits, scores the rubric, and reviews `docs/reports/M4.md`. Later milestones (`LATER.md`) are planned with the owner before starting.
