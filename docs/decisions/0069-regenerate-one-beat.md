# 0069 — Regenerating one beat around its locked shots

- Status: accepted (M4 step 3b)
- Deciders: agent (autonomous; no human gate)

## Context

PRODUCT.md §8 lets the owner regenerate the whole edit, the story only, or one beat, and every action respects locks. M4 acceptance 2: after the owner locks 3 shots and regenerates their beat, the locked shots stay unchanged at the same relative positions. ADR 0068 made a lock pin the shot's cut. Until now the only regeneration was a full `edit.generate`, which starts again from the planner and keeps no lock.

## Decision

- **Scope of the new take.** A beat is regenerated by a new prompt, `reselect` v1, under the `selector` capability (provider and model are the selector's).
  - It sees the beat's slots in order, with the locked shots shown as fixed. It also sees the beat's pool, minus the clips the edit uses in other beats and the ones the owner took out (`avoid`).
  - It answers with new selections for the free slots only, in order. Decisions only: no timing (invariant 5).
- **Validation (invariant 6).** Every pick must come from that pool and be used once. Alternatives must also come from the pool. There must be at least as many picks as free slots before the last locked shot, so every locked shot keeps its index. An answer that fails is retried with the errors, then fails the task.
- **Fixed and free slots.**
  - Locked shots, and picks absorbed into a locked cut (ADR 0068), keep their index in the beat. This is the "same relative position" of acceptance 2.
  - The new picks fill the free slots in order, and any extra picks are appended. They get new refs in the beat's numbering (`b2#7`, …), so they never take over an old pick's ref.
  - Other beats, the excluded list and the owner's order flag are unchanged.
- **A fresh take each time.** The AI call's variant is the beat's take number (how many times it was regenerated, plus one) within the edit's own request variant. Regenerating it again asks again, and two takes of an edit (`--variant`) get their own fresh takes, while a retry of the same take replays the cache. The key, `edit.beat`, covers:
  - the base version's key, the beat and the take;
  - the candidates and the rejected segments;
  - the selector's provider and model, and the prompt version.
- **It runs as a job.** `POST /edits/{eid}/beats/{bid}/regenerate` returns 202 with a job (invariant 8).
  - **Checked before anything is saved.** It is refused with 422 when every shot of the beat is locked, and with 404 for an unknown beat. A refused request saves nothing.
  - **Then a changed draft is saved.** It is saved when the job is queued, and again when the job starts.
  - **Before any AI call,** the job also refuses a beat whose pool has fewer clips than the free slots before its last locked shot, with a reason. No answer could fill such a beat.
  - **The new version.** The job times the new storyboard with `resolve` and commits it as an `ai` version, "Regenerated “title”", with the selector's provider, model, cost and cache use in `metrics.ai` and the take in `metrics.regenerated`. Its export is written.
  - **When the owner keeps editing.** S16 keeps the other beats editable meanwhile. If the storyboard changes while the AI answers, the commit is refused, and the job saves the change and redoes the take, up to 3 times. An unchanged prompt replays the cache, so the redo costs no second call. Then it fails with a reason.
  - **The shimmer's source.** `GET /edits/{eid}/draft` lists the beats with a regeneration still running (`regenerating`, from the jobs), so S16's shimmer survives a reload.
- **Regenerating the story or the whole edit while keeping locks** moves to M4 step 4, with the other regeneration actions. Those need the locked shots carried into a new plan, whose beats may differ.

## Consequences

- Acceptance 2 runs in `make ci` with the fake adapter, which answers `reselect` with a different take where the pool allows. It checks that each locked shot keeps its pin, its slot and its relative order, that other beats are unchanged, and that no vision call is made.
- In S16 (M4 step 14c), a beat being regenerated shimmers while the others stay interactive.
