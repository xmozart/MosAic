## What was built

- **Foundation.**
  - Exact time types: integer ticks and frames, Fractions, property-tested.
  - Control and project databases with Alembic migrations, a placement policy, a project
    descriptor, and an idempotent artifact store with provenance on every row.
  - A job DAG with leases, heartbeats, resume after `kill -9`, cancel, defer and cost
    reservation, run by one worker process with a thread per resource class.
  - FastAPI (`/api/jobs`, settings, providers, secrets, edits, renders) with SSE.
- **Media and analysis (L0–L2).**
  - Scan and probe with camera profiles (iPhone, GoPro, generic): chapters, LRF sidecars,
    unsupported reasons, deferred photos.
  - 720p CFR SDR proxies with tone mapping, rotation, colour range, VFR→CFR and a tick map.
  - A numpy port of the adaptive shot detector; samples with pHash/SigLIP dedupe;
    sharpness, exposure, noise, obstruction, shake and freeze metrics; GPMF gyro.
  - VAD and faster-whisper word ticks, plus loudness.
  - SigLIP ONNX embeddings with sqlite-vec indexes; segments with `usable_range`;
    similarity groups.
  - Labelled mosaics sized to the provider, the vision v1 prompt, and USE/MAYBE/REJECT
    dispositions in which user decisions survive re-segmentation.
- **AI layer.**
  - A provider registry with app-config providers, keys only in the OS keychain, a
    validated and cached `AIClient`, and atomic cost budgets.
  - Adapters: Anthropic API, **Claude Code CLI** and **Codex CLI** (the owner's request:
    no API key; ADR 0014), and an offline fake/replay adapter.
  - A `mosaic config` CLI with presets (`mosaic config ai use …`).
- **Editing.**
  - Capped retrieval; planner v1 and selector v1 with code-checked references.
  - A deterministic solver; a cut refiner (words, sentences, motion settle, frame grid);
    a jump-cut fixer with backfill.
  - Critic metrics, immutable versions with a JSON export, `mosaic edit` and
    `mosaic report`.
- **Render.**
  - Per-event chunks: cuts across chapters, phase-exact nearest-frame conform (HFR/VFR),
    tone mapping, pillarbox, gain and fades, silence fill, and verified frame and sample
    counts.
  - Constant-frame-rate stream-copy assembly with two-pass loudnorm.
  - Preview, final and lossless profiles; `mosaic render`.
- **Evaluation.**
  - An acceptance suite and a `make eval` runner with G1/G2 gates and run/milestone
    budgets.
  - Drafted expectations, a rubric template, and a ledger.

## Problems found by the real-corpus run (fixed)

- **Sample extraction failed on real footage.** 120-term FFmpeg `select` expressions
  exceeded the parser's limit. The fix caps a call at 48 frames, and the builder refuses
  more than 64. Commit `2952096`.
- **The final render's AAC sample check was wrong.** The container duration counts
  priming and padding. The check now counts decoded samples with `astats`.
- **Rejections were too aggressive.** The AI's bare "not usable" now means MAYBE, after
  Haiku rejected airshow sky shots containing distant aircraft. ADR 0013 is amended.
- **NVENC listed but unusable.** The first CI run failed because Linux FFmpeg lists NVENC
  and the runner has no NVIDIA GPU. Encoders are now chosen by a trial encode
  (commit "M0.12d").
- **Wrong served model in provenance.** The Claude CLI also lists its helper models, so
  the requested model now wins in provenance.

## Known gaps and open questions

- The thresholds and similarity distance are uncalibrated (ADR 0001 items 4–5).
- Trip context, music, the AI critic, the UI and photos in edits are out of M0 scope.
- `docs/OPEN_QUESTIONS.md` Q-1: a commercial licensing review of the bundled runtime
  libraries (ADR 0010).
- A job paused at its cost limit cannot yet have its limit raised and be resumed; a new
  run reuses its work.
- Artifact garbage collection: old artifacts are kept when keys change. The Airshow
  workspace is 3.2 GB.
