## Try it

```bash
make desktop-dev        # or: open the web UI as before
```

1. Open a trip, then go to **Edits → Create edit** (S14). There are six steps: length and format, story (presets or your own beats), what to feature, style, sound (with a music track), and anything else. The summary on the right shows time, AI cost and whether the footage is enough.
2. **Create** opens S15. You see the steps and the beats as they are planned, then the preview renders. **View storyboard** opens S16.
3. In **S16**:
   - Drag shots within and between beats, and drag beats to reorder them.
   - Click a shot for why it was chosen and what could replace it, with Lock, Remove and Play.
   - Each beat has Regenerate and Faster (Slower is in ⋯).
   - Keys: ⌘Z / ⇧⌘Z undo and redo, Space plays, L locks, Delete removes.
   - **Save version** keeps your changes, and **Preview** saves first.
4. In **S17**:
   - The findings are on the right. Apply or Ignore each one, or Apply all. "Version N created · Undo" follows each fix.
   - **Regenerate with fixes** applies every fix, then regenerates the beats whose issues have no fix.
   - **Versions** (S18) compares two versions side by side with synced players and lists what changed. **Revert** is there too.
   - **Render final** (S19) offers Web H.264, High quality HEVC or Master ProRes 422, with size and time estimates. You can also add SRT, the selection report or JSON, and **Save to** a folder of your choice.
5. In the **Library**, after analysis, **Help me label** (S24) asks up to five optional questions (where a day was, whether clips show the same person, what happened). Answers go into the trip details or link clips. Nothing about faces is stored.

## What was built

- **The edit request v2** (ADR 0066):
  - arc and custom beats, content weights, editing rules, pace and shot lengths;
  - transitions, photo share, audio options, loudness, and a music bed.
- **Drafts and versions** (ADR 0067):
  - Every storyboard change is an undoable op on a draft, timed by the solver with no AI.
  - Saving makes an immutable version, and a version is saved before every render or regenerate.
- **Locks** (ADR 0068):
  - A lock pins the shot's exact source range and length.
  - Locks are kept through every later change: regenerate a beat (acceptance 2), the story or the whole edit, length changes and fixes.
  - Locks that can't fit are reported in frames ("Your locked shots run 0:40 past the longest this edit can be").
- **Regeneration at every level** (ADR 0069–0074):
  - One beat, the story, the whole edit, a stronger opening, a different ending, more or less of a category.
  - "Make it N seconds" uses no AI (acceptance 3).
  - Faster or slower per beat, and a shot's alternatives.
- **Content categories and planner/selector v3** (ADR 0072–0073): weights steer retrieval, and your beats, rules and sound choices reach the prompts.
- **Photos in edits** (ADR 0075):
  - Bursts and capture twins are handled, and the photo share is respected.
  - Stills play 2–5 s with pan and zoom.
  - Orientation is fixed, and a portrait photo in a landscape edit gets a blurred fill built with Pillow; FFmpeg's `boxblur` is GPL (acceptance 4).
- **Subject-aware framing** (ADR 0076): saliency, not faces, sets the crop for non-native aspect ratios and where still motion goes.
- **Cut refiner v2** (ADR 0077): J- and L-cuts into and out of dialogue (up to 1 s, in frames), speech-aware in and out points, and separate audio chunks.
- **Transitions** (ADR 0078): dissolves and dips to black, rendered as their own chunks.
- **Title cards** (ADR 0079): the opening, day and closing cards use text only from your trip details and capture dates, drawn with Pillow in Geist.
- **Music bed** (ADR 0080): your track with fades, a static level, and −12 dB ducking under detected speech.
- **Critic and findings** (ADR 0081–0082):
  - Deterministic checks, with fixes written as storyboard ops.
  - An optional AI critic reads the cut and sends notes by event id, never timecodes.
  - Each applied fix makes a child version.
- **Compare and revert** (ADR 0083): reverting makes a new version, and its render reuses the earlier file byte for byte (acceptance 5).
- **Final renders** (ADR 0084, 0091):
  - H.264 and HEVC on hardware encoders, and ProRes 422 (VideoToolbox, else `prores_ks`), at 720p to 4K.
  - Loudness Web −14 or TV −23 LUFS.
  - SRT, the selection report and the edit JSON as extra files.
  - Save to a folder (desktop): files are copied whole, never over an existing file, and never into your footage.
- **Help me label** (ADR 0085, 0092): questions come from what analysis knows. Answers become trip-context records and `person_link` rows only (acceptance 7).
- **Screens** (ADR 0086–0093): S13 full, S14 full, S15, S16, S17 findings, S18, S19 and S24. Every listed state has a story in both themes (acceptance 6).
