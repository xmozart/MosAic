## What was built

- **The owner's G6 feedback first (Airshow: more aircraft and the F-35 demo, fewer crowds).**
  - `trip_context`: imported from JSON or free text with `mosaic context`. The planner and selector use it, and so do summaries. Names come only from context (invariant 16).
  - L3 deep review: full-resolution frames of candidate segments, reviewed by the `reviewer` capability (Sonnet 5.5). It can be scoped to the project, a day or a selection, under a cost ceiling that pauses and resumes.
  - Airshow re-run with context and Thorough mode: by primary subject, aircraft went from 45 % to **79 %** of edit time and crowd from 29 % to **2 %**, with 0 must-exclude violations.
- **Analysis modes** Quick, Balanced, Thorough and Custom (ADR 0020):
  - Each job carries its mode. Every stage's key includes its mode parameters, and the Balanced keys are M0's.
  - Quick uses LRF proxies (frame-accurate) or 540p and the threshold detector.
  - Scoped deepening adds L2 where it is missing, then L3.
  - Estimates come from the probe and, since step 12, from this computer's benchmark.
- **Hierarchical summaries** go from shot to scene to day to trip (ADR 0021). A context change re-runs summaries only.
- **Storage placement** (ADR 0022):
  - `split` for network, cloud and read-only folders: the live DB and cache live in app data, and backup-API snapshots go into the folder.
  - `external` for read-only folders.
  - Cloud-placeholder detection; relocation; project locks.
- **Project lifecycle** (ADR 0023): reopening relinks files by content fingerprint, with no re-analysis. It also handles new, missing and changed files, and project leases with read-only open and take-over.
- **Cameras** (ADR 0024):
  - `insta360`: flat footage is full; raw 360 is `analysis_only` through a forward view.
  - `dji`: chapters, LRF and SRT. Also `nikon_z` and the GoPro timelapse flag.
  - An unsupported-reason catalog with suggested fixes.
- **Photos** (ADRs 0025, 0026):
  - JPEG and HEIC (decode-only, LGPL), and NEF, DNG, ARW and CR2 through their embedded previews.
  - Live Photo pairing; photos as one-frame clips through embeddings, sheets, vision and dispositions.
  - Burst groups, and photo-plus-video moments.
- **Clock and colour** (ADRs 0027, 0028):
  - Devices with raw and corrected capture times; offsets apply without re-analysis.
  - Clock-offset suggestions from cross-device evidence; `mosaic clock`.
  - A per-device `.cube` LUT for log footage, applied in proxies and final renders.
- **Hybrid search** (ADR 0029): SigLIP text-to-image search plus FTS5 over descriptions, tags and transcripts, merged by reciprocal rank fusion. Available through the API, `mosaic search` and suggestions. Queries are read-only and never download a model.
- **Hardware and workers** (ADR 0030):
  - A hardware probe and a benchmark measured on a generated 4K clip through the real proxy builder, frame pass and embedder.
  - Estimates based on the benchmark; worker slots sized from the probe.
  - Per-provider AI rate limits.
- **Scale** (ADR 0031): the 40-hour synthetic project, and `GET /projects/{pid}/library` with keyset pages by day or camera (the paging core of S10).

## Problems found by the eval (fixed)

- **Upgrading an existing project could fail and leave a partial schema** (M1.14a).
  - The M1.10a migration rebuilds `asset`. SQLite refused to drop it while foreign keys were on and other rows referenced it.
  - Worse, Python's `sqlite3` driver had already committed an earlier `CREATE TABLE` outside the transaction, so the failed upgrade left one empty, unstamped table (`asset_stage`) in the Airshow project.
  - Migrations now run in one real transaction that includes DDL, with foreign keys off and a `foreign_key_check` before the commit.
  - New tests upgrade populated databases from the M0, M1.4 and M1.9b schemas, and check that a failed upgrade leaves nothing behind.
  - Every earlier test had migrated only empty databases, which is why this was missed.

- **Library list.** Day grouping uses the corrected time's stored offset; M2's S10 work adds filters, density and `similar`.
